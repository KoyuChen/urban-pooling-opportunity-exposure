# Claim boundary

## Supported

For the adaptively selected released 15-minute core, the extraction is
metadata/count stable and count-closed over every literal public
`Shared Trip Match=true, Trips Pooled=2` row that can be incident to a core
trip under the declared nearest-15-minute release model. The result is a
boundary-complete public temporal candidate superset for core-incident edges.

The radius and Gamma families are nested. Eight of ten complete curve/query
chains, covering four of five query families, have paired optimal numerical
endpoints and pass the widening audit with zero mathematical violations.

The separate live release-operator audit reconciles the 611 candidates against
50,405 all-trip endpoint-bin contributors. The closed outer released-time-
envelope graph has 24,274 intersections. Removing 5,812 boundary-touch-only
intersections leaves a positive-length outer-envelope graph with 18,462 edges.
Conditional on that graph, two core covers are certified, and the displayed
pair differs on all 60 core assignments. This is graph-cover multiplicity, not
two complete latent Chicago worlds. No hidden-partner identification or
nonidentification theorem is claimed.

## Not supported

Chicago does not release Shared Trip ID or co-rider identity in these rows.
The analysis therefore does not establish actual trip overlap, hidden-run or
recursive buffer-run closure, identify or prove nonidentification of true
partners, estimate partner recall, validate Chicago conformal coverage, or
establish a city-population effect. Released distance, duration, fare,
centroid, and area fields are not latent exact trip attributes.

The 2 km radius is an analyst sensitivity screen, not a proven necessary
support rule. Gamma counts measured out-of-radius core incidences only;
unmeasured-distance edges are retained with zero Gamma cost. The release audit
finds zero operator-licensed deletions among those edges. Within the positive-
length outer released-time-envelope graph-cover feasible set, the sharp
spatially unmeasured core-incidence range is `[15,60]`. Public blanks emit no
LOW tract-count literals, candidate mask equality is snapshot-descriptive only,
and City implementation fidelity is not claimed.
