# Claim boundary

## Supported

For one adaptively selected released 15-minute core, the extraction is
metadata/count stable and count-closed over every literal public
`Shared Trip Match=true, Trips Pooled=2` row that can form a core-incident
outer temporal edge under the declared nearest-15-minute release model.

The boundary-padding family is nested. Its complete endpoint is
`p=2 delta=15` minutes, and every `p>15` point is graph- and endpoint-identical
under the declared model. The live boundary identity audit passes with zero
mismatches. In this core, completing the boundary expands the graph by 12.15%
but expands miles- and duration-gap widths by only 0.53% and 1.02%.

The radius and Gamma families are also nested. Twelve of fifteen complete
curve/query chains, covering four of five query families, have paired optimal
numerical endpoints and pass the widening audit with zero mathematical
violations. Every published endpoint has MIP gap zero and zero replay residual.

The separate release-operator audit remains complementary: it concerns
released-field-map and graph-cover ambiguity, not recovered partners. Its
positive-length outer-envelope graph results are conditional graph statements,
not completed latent Chicago worlds.

## Not supported

Chicago does not release Shared Trip ID, co-rider identity, or vehicle identity.
The analysis therefore does not establish actual positive-duration overlap,
hidden-run or recursive buffer-run closure, identify or prove nonidentification
of the true partners for this cohort, estimate partner recall, validate the
City's private implementation, or establish a Chicago-population effect.

Padding below 15 minutes is an intentional under-padding stress test and has no
partner-recall interpretation. Padding above 15 minutes does not make the
buffer recursively run-closed; it adds no core-incident public temporal edge
under the stated rounding model.

The 2 km radius is an analyst sensitivity screen, not a proven necessary
compatibility rule. Gamma counts measured out-of-radius core incidences only;
unmeasured-distance edges are retained with zero Gamma cost. Gamma is neither a
total candidate-miss budget nor an estimated miss rate.

Released distance, duration, fare, centroid, and community-area fields are not
latent exact trip attributes. Six rows lack public fare, so all fare chains are
fail-closed. Pickup and dropoff community-area ranges remain `[0,1]`.
