\paragraph{Chicago $K=2$ follow-up.}
Of 96 declared windows, 90 completed and six were scientifically ineligible;
none had an execution failure or remained unstarted. Among 10,762 data-complete
endpoint pairs, 10,728 (99.684\%) were numerically certified, while 34 remained
computationally unresolved; a further 2,628 pairs lacked public query values.
One window was completed under a declared audit amendment: an apparent decrease
of 0.000641 minutes per core was smaller than its 0.000962-minute objective
allowance implied by the reported MIP gap, and was retained as numerically
indeterminate rather than called monotone. These fixed-calendar windows are not
a probability sample, hidden-partner recovery study, or Chicago population
estimate.
