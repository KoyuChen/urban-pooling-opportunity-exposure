# Decision certification versus full relation recovery

Among 5,954 ambiguous selected-mean comparisons, the minimum decision certificate has mean **1.56** facts, versus **3.49** to identify the complete selected set. Strict savings occur in **95.1%** of cells; the mean decision/full ratio is **0.452**.

Among 272 ambiguous event-count instances, the minimum decision certificate has mean **2.01** pair facts, versus **2.64** to identify the complete partition. Strict savings occur in **52.6%** of cells; the mean decision/full ratio is **0.772**.

The comparison is exact for the controlled small instances and does not assign a real-world cost to a relation query.
