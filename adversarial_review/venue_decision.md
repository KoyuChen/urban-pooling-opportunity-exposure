# Venue Decision

## Decision

**Current manuscript: NO-GO for submission.**

**Research program: CONDITIONAL GO**, with *Data Science for Transportation* as
the first attainable target after a complete, suppression-aware Chicago study.
*Transportation Research Part C* is a stretch only if the coupled
suppression–geography–matching problem yields general theory and a scalable
certified algorithm. *Transportation Research Part A* is an alternative only
if the 2026 policy analysis passes its legal-treatment, support, pretrend, and
uncertainty gates and produces a substantive policy conclusion.

KDD Research, KDD AI for Sciences, and *Transportation Science* are not
supported by the current evidence or by the elementary missing-bin/global-OLS
repairs alone.

## Why the current paper cannot be submitted honestly

1. Its primary observational interpretation contradicts the official
   `Shared Trip Match` definition.
2. The current candidate graph is not a coverage graph and can delete true
   co-present partners by day, distance, and degree rules.
3. Complete-case geography can create feasible but wholly false pairings.
4. The node objective is not a joint latent-edge likelihood and does not
   identify edge rankings.
5. The raw `rho` radius is scale-dependent and equal values are not comparable
   across score maps.
6. The optimization correctly gives attained extremal values, but the closed
   interval between them is only the convex hull of the generally discrete
   attainable set; calling that whole interval the exact attainable set is
   wrong.
7. The synthetic primary was selected after detecting circularity in the
   original generator/specification and remains proxy-rich.
8. The current downloader cannot estimate `/all` policy denominators.
9. The declared policy block optimization is separable; current ITT language
   is invalid.
10. No complete-day Chicago structural estimate, production scalability
    result, or policy estimate exists.

## Submission gates

### Gate A: coherent scientific object

- Use customer transactions, not riders.
- Target realized two-transaction co-presence composition, not platform
  opportunity.
- Define (K=2) scope and boundary-complete population explicitly.

### Gate B: observation and candidate support

- Verify the current 2025/2026 suppression and legal tax operators.
- Download all trips for suppression/policy cells and boundary-buffered matched
  rows for linkage.
- Build a necessary-condition coverage graph; treat spatial screens/caps as
  sensitivities.
- Reject conflicting IDs and quarantine unresolved boundary components.

### Gate C: method

- Exact-cover all core target nodes before context handling.
- Model suppression-coupled tract assignments or clearly label simpler bounds
  as independent-support baselines.
- Report attained endpoint bounds/convex-hull ranges.
- Use a composite-score interpretation; keep score restrictions optional and
  calibrated externally.
- Separate exact/exhaustive certificates from floating-point numerical optima,
  and distinguish infeasible, bounded-gap, timeout, and error states.

### Gate D: validation

- Freeze a new untouched benchmark family.
- Include realistic misspecification, coarsening, suppression, boundary, and
  omitted-edge stress tests.
- Obtain independent pair truth or present all edge restrictions strictly as
  sensitivity assumptions.
- Compare with community-area aggregation, complete cases, independent-label
  bounds, single imputation, DBSCAN proxy grouping, and score-free
  endpoints.

### Gate E: policy

- Use all reported transactions or an external stable denominator.
- Encode both endpoints, old/new zones, weekday/weekend schedules, and timing
  ambiguity.
- Optimize a fixed endpoint-level contrast over one global feasible-world
  model when geography, treatment, membership, or denominators vary; do not
  call transaction composition a traveler ITT.
- Add valid sampling inference for optimized endpoints and all standard causal
  diagnostics.

## Claim ladder after repair

| Evidence achieved | Maximum honest claim |
|---|---|
| Formal propositions and exhaustive toy tests only | A conditional measurement method with counterexamples |
| Complete Chicago run but no pair truth | Assumption-indexed contextual endpoint ranges among reported (K=2) transactions |
| Independent candidate/score truth | Empirically validated tightening for the audited markets only |
| One-signed robust policy range plus causal diagnostics | Policy-induced change in a precisely defined reported-transaction outcome, not general traveler exposure |
| General coupled algorithm plus transfer or benchmark evidence | Potential KDD Research method claim |
| New general optimization/identification theory or one exceptional actionable transportation result | Potential Transportation Science claim |

## Recommended path

Prioritize the mechanism-conditional suppression-aware measurement paper and target *Data
Science for Transportation*. Defer KDD and policy branding. Reopen TR-C only
after the coupled formulation is provably nontrivial, computationally scalable,
and materially changes the Chicago conclusions.
