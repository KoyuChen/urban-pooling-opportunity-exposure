\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Uncertain linkage and aggregate queries.}
Record-linkage research represents globally compatible links as constrained
matchings rather than independent pair decisions \citep{sadinle2017linkage}.
Database work propagates linkage uncertainty to aggregate queries
\citep{hua2012aggregate} and optimizes extremal aggregate answers over
candidate-constrained two-table integrations \citep{turkcapar2023aggregate}.
Attribute-uncertain databases likewise track value bounds through relational
queries \citep{feng2021audb}. We share the possible-worlds principle but study a
different latent object: rows inside one event stream must be partitioned into
temporally ordered groups whose total membership may exceed simultaneous
capacity, while exact timestamps may themselves be set-valued. The estimand is
an aggregate frontier over joint grouping and time completions, not a posterior
over pair identities.

\paragraph{Temporal grouping and mobility applications.}
Shareability networks and dynamic trip--vehicle assignment solve a forward
allocation problem when requests and candidate trips are observed
\citep{santi2014shareability,alonsomora2017ondemand,ozkanward2020dynamic}.
Empirical work predicts sharing or matching outcomes
\citep{taiebat2022sharing,mucci2024understanding}, studies pooled demand across
neighborhoods \citep{soria2021sociospatial}, or constructs proxy groups by
spatiotemporal clustering \citep{sebtichen2026hidden}. Our city audit is
inverse: the release preserves transaction rows and outcomes but removes the
event key. More broadly, the same inferential object arises in referral,
household-visit, and service-tour streams. We do not select the most likely
cluster; we certify which downstream aggregate claims survive all declared
temporal-event decompositions.

\paragraph{Structured optimization.}
Integer programming is standard for globally coupled disclosure constraints
\citep{fischetti2001suppression}, and bounded-width dynamic programming is
standard in structured inference \citep{freuder1990ktree,dechter1999bucket}.
Our decomposition exploits a more specific interval structure. One rooted
event with a fixed span has an integral consecutive-ones formulation, which
becomes an exact pricing oracle for a Dantzig--Wolfe master. The master itself
is nonintegral, so we use branch-compatible row-usage and pair-co-membership
disjunctions. This separates a polynomial local pricing problem from a
potentially exponential global integer search while retaining valid anytime
bounds.

\paragraph{Candidate restrictions and calibrated learning.}
Learning can sharpen a feasible-world set, but node-level event indicators do
not identify partner probabilities without assumptions. Matching-level
conformal prediction and weakly supervised structured prediction have
well-developed foundations \citep{lei2018conformal,cauchois2024weak}. We keep
learned scoring outside the central contribution. Headline candidate rules are
score-free and outcome-blind; the controlled study instead asks whether simple
point reconstructions yield decisions invariant to the certified frontier and
how candidate truncation affects full-world coverage.
