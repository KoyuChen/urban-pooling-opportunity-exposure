\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Uncertain linkage and aggregate queries.}
Record-linkage research represents globally compatible links as constrained
matchings rather than independent pair decisions \citep{sadinle2017linkage}.
Database work has propagated linkage uncertainty to aggregate queries
\citep{hua2012aggregate} and optimized extremal aggregate answers over
candidate-constrained two-table integrations \citep{turkcapar2023aggregate}.
Attribute-uncertain databases likewise track value bounds through relational
queries \citep{feng2021audb}. We share the possible-worlds viewpoint but study
a different latent object: rows in one public event stream must be partitioned
into temporally ordered groups whose total membership may exceed simultaneous
capacity. The release can also expose only a support for each exact timestamp.
Our estimand is an aggregate frontier over all such groupings and time
completions, not a posterior over pair identities.

\paragraph{Ride pooling and temporal grouping.}
Shareability networks and dynamic trip--vehicle assignment solve a forward
allocation problem when requests and candidate trips are observed
\citep{santi2014shareability,alonsomora2017ondemand,ozkanward2020dynamic}.
Empirical work predicts sharing or matching outcomes
\citep{taiebat2022sharing,mucci2024understanding}, studies pooled demand across
neighborhoods \citep{soria2021sociospatial}, or constructs proxy groups by
spatiotemporal clustering \citep{sebtichen2026hidden}. Our problem is inverse:
the release preserves trip rows and outcomes but removes the event key. We do
not select a most likely cluster. We certify what aggregate conclusions survive
across every latent run decomposition admitted by declared public information.

\paragraph{Structured optimization and disclosure constraints.}
Integer programming is standard for globally coupled disclosure-control
constraints \citep{fischetti2001suppression}, and bounded-width dynamic
programming is standard in structured inference
\citep{freuder1990ktree,dechter1999bucket}. Our algorithmic contribution is
specific to ordered interval runs. For one rooted run and a fixed span,
positive-overlap connectivity and occupancy constraints yield an integral
interval LP. This becomes an exact pricing oracle for a Dantzig--Wolfe master.
The coupling master is nevertheless nonintegral, which separates the tractable
single-run subproblem from the unresolved integer decomposition problem.

\paragraph{Candidate restrictions and calibrated learning.}
Learning may sharpen a feasible-world set, but node-level sharing outcomes do
not identify partner probabilities without assumptions. Matching-level
conformal prediction and weakly supervised structured prediction already have
well-developed foundations \citep{lei2018conformal,cauchois2024weak}. We
therefore keep learned scoring outside the central contribution of this paper.
The main results are score-free and expose candidate-support, timestamp-support,
and capacity assumptions separately.
