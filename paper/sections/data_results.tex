\section{Public-Data Audit Design}
\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Complementary releases.}
Chicago's TNP release provides a declared two-transaction shared-trip benchmark
with nearest-15-minute public times and privacy-coarsened geography
\citep{chicagodata2026,chicagomanual2026,chicagoprivacy2026}. NYC's High Volume
For-Hire Vehicle release provides second-level pickup and drop-off times,
shared-match indicators, Taxi Zones, trip miles, and trip time, but no public
co-rider or run key. We use the official 2023 Open Data table\footnote{Dataset
\texttt{u253-aew4}: \url{https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/u253-aew4}.} for
audited exact optimization and the May 2026 monthly Parquet only for a current
candidate-multiplicity audit. Neither release supplies operational event
membership truth.

\paragraph{Candidate universes and frozen designs.}
A candidate rule is part of the estimand rather than hidden preprocessing.
Chicago uses a boundary-complete core-incident temporal universe and reports
radius and omitted-incidence sensitivity separately. NYC uses provider and
temporal proximity; exact Taxi Zone equality is only a sensitivity screen
because it can delete plausible relations. No public outcome enters headline
candidate construction. The frozen NYC decision panel predeclares 24 windows:
20 eight-core windows across four seasons, weekday/weekend regimes, and
morning, midday, or evening periods, plus four 16-core weekday-evening stress
cells. Each eligible window is evaluated for two outcomes and
$C\in\{2,3,4\}$. The integer scale lattice predeclares six core/buffer sizes
from $4+12$ through $16+48$ at the same capacities. Eligibility is determined
before outcomes are optimized.

\paragraph{Solver and disclosure contracts.}
Exact fixed-time frontiers use complete enumeration only where declared.
Column generation closes the full LP after every rooted pricing problem has no
improving column. Branch-and-price calls an integer optimum only after the node
queue closes; otherwise it retains an incumbent and open-node upper bound.
Continuous cells use solver certificates and witness replay. Technical failure,
scientific ineligibility, unresolved optimization, and infeasibility are never
conflated. Artifacts publish aggregate tables, hashes, and statuses, but no raw
identifiers, event columns, partner assignments, or latent timestamps.

\section{Public-Data Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Fine timestamps do not identify the hidden relation}
The May 2026 NYC file contains 222,149 public shared-match rows. In a fixed
200-row cohort, exact public interval overlap yields 154,708 directed
core-candidate incidences over 1,042 candidate nodes; the median core degree is
786. Requiring equal pickup and drop-off Taxi Zones leaves only 44 incidences.
Zone equality is therefore an aggressive candidate screen, not a defensible
necessary condition for hidden co-membership.

Table~\ref{tab:pairwise} uses a separate fixed 2023 pairwise cohort. Exact
seconds already leave broad ranges. Artificial 15-minute time coarsening adds
79.5\% more candidate edges and increases the three displayed identified
widths by 12.4--15.3\%. Time coarsening aggravates ambiguity but does not create
it.

\subsection{Capacity uncertainty is front-loaded}
At a common support of 72 selected buffers, miles width nearly doubles from
$C=2$ to 3 but rises only 6.0\% from $C=3$ to 4; duration behaves similarly.
The first relaxation accounts for 89.2\% of total miles widening and 86.2\% of
duration widening through $C=4$ (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:capacitydetail}). This
is cohort-specific identification, not realized capacity. At each capacity's
own maximum support, ranges may instead contract because the conditioning event
forces more rows; fixed support restores Proposition~\ref{prop:nesting}.

\subsection{Point decisions are usually not relation-certifiable}
All 24 predeclared NYC windows return terminal reports. Twenty-one are
scientifically eligible; three outcome-blind windows fail to produce an
integrity- and cap-qualified core; technical failures are zero. The eligible
windows yield 126 outcome--capacity cells. Table~\ref{tab:decisionpanel} shows
that 101 endpoint pairs close exactly. At the candidate-median threshold, 125
cells contain attained feasible values on both sides and are therefore
certified ambiguous; the remaining 16-core miles cell is unresolved rather
than forced into a conclusion.

The point methods---nearest-time and zone-aware pairings plus temporal-score and
zone-score ordered decompositions---produce 498 capacity-indexed decisions;
494 (99.2\%) lie in cells where the relation-invariant side is not certified.
The 19.0\% figure is disagreement \emph{among} point methods, not disagreement
with observed truth. In all 102 eight-core cells, ambiguity persists at the
candidate first quartile, median, and third quartile, so the median result is
not generated by one threshold choice.

\subsection{Exact integer decomposition has a visible scale boundary}
A predeclared 18-cell lattice closes every integer optimum through $16+48$ at
$C=2,3,4$ (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:scaledetail}). The four initially open cells
retain their anytime intervals in the audit history, then close at 30, 36, 44,
and 48 under longer budgets. This is an exact medium-instance path, not a
city-scale runtime guarantee; no timeout is relabeled as optimality.

\subsection{Chicago extraction is count-closed, not identity-complete}
The repaired Chicago audit uses a narrow overlap index followed by 14 exact
released-start shards and 14/13 contributor start/end shards. It obtains 60
core rows, 611 K=2 temporal candidates, and all 50,405 public trips contributing
to their endpoint bins. Counts close before and after extraction, no candidate
has a null time endpoint, and the public snapshot remains stable.

Among 24,274 closed outer-envelope intersections, 18,462 have positive length
and 5,812 only touch at a boundary. On the positive-length graph, two optimal
core covers are certified with zero reported MIP gap and replay residual; all
60 core assignments differ. This demonstrates conditional graph-cover
multiplicity. It does \emph{not} construct common exact-time witnesses, pair all
remaining buffers, validate provider/vehicle feasibility, or produce two full
Chicago hidden-run worlds. The audit therefore retains the status
\texttt{PARTIAL\_DOCUMENTED\_PUBLIC\_CONSISTENCY}, emits no inferred LOW tract
literals, and never interprets a public blank as a privacy cell without
independent evidence.
