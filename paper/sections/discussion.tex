\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Knowledge discovery under missing relations.}
The method changes the target from ``recover one plausible relation'' to
``certify which downstream statements are invariant to the admissible
relations.'' The controlled experiments show why this is not a semantic
distinction: a fully feasible point reconstruction can cross the wrong policy
threshold, while the frontier correctly marks the conclusion as ambiguous.
Conversely, when the entire frontier lies on one side, every feasible world
agrees. This certified-decision view is portable to referral episodes,
household or field visits, service tours, and other row-complete streams with
hidden temporal grouping.

\paragraph{What is identified.}
Endpoints are sharp or certified only relative to the declared candidate
universe, timestamp supports, connectivity rule, capacity, and query. They do
not identify a unique partition. Expanding timestamp support or capacity cannot
shrink a fixed-support feasible set, but comparisons at capacity-specific
maximum support change the conditioning event. Candidate truncation is more
fundamental: if a true event is deleted before optimization, endpoint
certification cannot restore it. The controlled benchmark demonstrates that
high member recall, a nonempty frontier, and even scalar coverage can coexist
with omission of the true joint world.

\paragraph{Algorithmic boundary.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle} makes fixed-span event pricing polynomial, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:cg} certifies the full LP without enumerating all columns.
Proposition~\ref{prop:bp} supplies an exact integer path through
branch-compatible row and pair disjunctions. The full algorithm is not claimed
polynomial: the number of forced-in/forced-out pricing cases can grow
exponentially with branch depth. The public 4+12 audit is an independent
correctness check, not evidence of city-scale closure. The next scaling
question is empirical---how root gap, branching, and generated columns evolve
with temporal density and core size---and can be reported honestly through
anytime global gaps when optimality is not reached.

\paragraph{Baselines and external validity.}
Our point rules are deliberately simple and interpretable. They establish that
feasibility or temporal plausibility does not guarantee a relation-invariant
decision, but they are not a final contest against modern learned linkage. A
stronger application study would train a scoring model on a separate
ground-truth source, calibrate candidate recall without using target outcomes,
and compare its point answer with the certified frontier. Public Chicago and
NYC data cannot supply that operational ground truth. Their role is to audit
the ambiguity geometry induced by real release operators, not to estimate
partner-recovery accuracy.

\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

Neither city release exposes a public vehicle-run key. We therefore do not
claim actual co-riders, partner recall outside a declared candidate universe,
production matching logic, realized pool size, or physical vehicle capacity.
The artificial 15-minute NYC supports are an information-loss intervention,
not an assertion about the TLC observation operator. The six-window panel is
purposive and too small for a citywide prevalence or causal estimate. The
controlled generator validates method logic under known truth but does not
turn its error rates into operational accuracy estimates.

Numerical claims retain their certificate class. Complete enumeration is used
only where explicitly stated; branch-and-price optima require an exhausted
node queue; time-limited runs keep valid incumbent and open-node bounds.
Candidate support remains an analyst-facing assumption rather than a hidden
modeling choice.

The unit of analysis is a released transaction, not a stable person. Zones and
neighborhood attributes describe context, not individual socioeconomic status
or preferences. Extremal event assignments can create reidentification risk,
so the public artifact suppresses row identifiers, event columns, and latent
world witnesses and reports aggregate results and provenance metadata only.

Generative AI assisted drafting, code review, counterexample search, and

\section{Conclusion}
Row-complete data can remain relation-incomplete enough to reverse downstream
decisions. \methodname{} represents every admissible ordered temporal event,
then reports only aggregate conclusions that survive that uncertainty. The
identification geometry separates candidate support, timestamp support, and
capacity. The optimization geometry separates an integral local pricing
problem, a nonintegral global master, and an exact branch-and-price certificate.
Across 3,000 controlled instances, plausible feasible point answers make
systematic threshold errors that the frontier consistently flags as ambiguous;
candidate truncation further shows that row recall is not relational-world
coverage. Chicago and NYC audits demonstrate that these issues persist in
public event streams with complementary release regimes. The resulting object
is not a recovered partner graph, but a certified boundary between discoverable
and relation-dependent aggregate knowledge.
