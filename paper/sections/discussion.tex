\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Knowledge discovery under missing relations.}
The method changes the target from ``recover one plausible relation'' to
``certify which downstream statements are invariant to admissible relations.''
The controlled experiments show why this is substantive: a fully feasible point
world can cross the wrong threshold, while the frontier marks the conclusion as
ambiguous. Conversely, when the entire frontier lies on one side, every feasible
world agrees. This certified-decision view is portable to referrals, household
or field visits, service tours, and other row-complete streams with hidden
temporal grouping.

\paragraph{What is identified.}
Endpoints are sharp or certified only relative to the declared candidate
universe, timestamp supports, connectivity rule, capacity, and query. They do
not identify a unique partition. Expanding timestamp support or capacity cannot
shrink a fixed-support feasible set, but comparisons at capacity-specific
maximum support change the conditioning event. Candidate truncation is more
fundamental: if a true event is deleted before optimization, endpoint
certification cannot restore it. The controlled benchmark demonstrates that
high member recall, a nonempty frontier, and even scalar coverage can coexist
with omission of the true joint world.

\paragraph{Decision ambiguity is not point-estimator accuracy.}
In the NYC panel, four feasible point methods disagree in 19.0\% of
outcome--capacity cells, while 494 of their 498 capacity-indexed decisions are
made inside certified-ambiguous cells. These facts show instability across
admissible point summaries; they do not reveal which point answer is correct,
because public memberships are absent. The frontier's role is to separate a
relation-invariant decision from a forced choice, not to retrospectively score
an unobserved ground truth.

\paragraph{Algorithmic boundary.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle} makes fixed-span event pricing polynomial, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:cg} certifies the full LP without enumerating all columns.
Proposition~\ref{prop:bp} supplies an exact integer path through
branch-compatible row and pair disjunctions. The full algorithm is not claimed
polynomial: forced-in/forced-out pricing cases can grow exponentially with
branch depth. The scale lattice closes all capacities through $8+24$ and 14 of
18 cells overall. The four larger unresolved cells keep valid gaps of one to
four. This is stronger than a toy correctness check but remains a
medium-instance result, not city-scale closure.

\paragraph{Public-release boundary.}
Chicago and NYC play complementary roles. NYC's second-level timestamps show
that fine time does not identify event membership. Chicago's successful sharded
transport establishes snapshot-relative count closure for candidates and all
endpoint-bin contributors. The two Chicago graph covers establish multiplicity
only inside the positive-length outer-envelope graph; they are not two fully
constructed operational worlds. Neither release validates provider matching
logic, hidden-run closure, realized capacity, or partner recall outside the
declared candidate universe.

\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

The 24-window NYC design is predeclared but purposive rather than probabilistic;
21 windows are eligible and three fail an outcome-blind integrity/cap screen.
The 16-core stress cells close only 10 of 24 endpoint pairs exactly, and one
median-threshold decision remains unresolved. These are disclosed computational
and design boundaries, not population estimates. The controlled generator
validates logic under known truth but does not turn its error rates into city
accuracy estimates.

Numerical claims retain their certificate class. Complete enumeration is used
only where stated; branch-and-price optima require an exhausted node queue;
time-limited runs keep valid incumbents and open-node bounds. Candidate support
remains an analyst-facing assumption rather than hidden preprocessing.

The unit of analysis is a released transaction, not a stable person. Zones and
neighborhood attributes describe context, not individual socioeconomic status
or preferences. Extremal assignments can create reidentification risk, so the
artifact suppresses row identifiers, event columns, and latent-world witnesses
and reports only aggregate results and provenance metadata.

\section{Generative AI Usage}
Generative AI assisted drafting, code review, counterexample search, and
editing. All mathematical claims, empirical outputs, citations, and artifact
states remain author-verified; generated text is not treated as city evidence.

\section{Conclusion}
Row-complete data can remain relation-incomplete enough to reverse downstream
decisions. \methodname{} represents admissible ordered temporal events and
reports only aggregate conclusions that survive that uncertainty. Across 3,000
controlled instances, feasible point answers make threshold errors that the
frontier always flags as ambiguous, while candidate truncation shows that row
recall is not relational-world coverage. In the NYC panel, 125 of 126
outcome--capacity cells are certified ambiguous at the candidate median and
point methods disagree in 19.0\% of cells; the scale lattice certifies 14 of 18
integer optima and bounds the rest. Chicago adds a repeatable count-closed
public extraction without converting graph-cover multiplicity into recovered
partners. The resulting object is not a preferred hidden graph, but a certified
boundary between discoverable and relation-dependent aggregate knowledge.
