\section{Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:nesting}}
Take $F=(t,\Pi)\in\F(\T,C,q)$. Since each $t_i\in\T_i\subseteq\T'_i$, the same
completion is allowed by $\T'$. Every run in $\Pi$ has the same positive-overlap
graph under the unchanged completion, and its maximum occupancy is at most
$C\le C'$. Core and buffer incidence constraints and the selected-buffer count
$q$ are unchanged. Hence $F\in\F(\T',C',q)$. Set inclusion immediately gives
nesting of reachable counts and maxima. Minimizing a fixed functional over a
superset can only weakly lower its optimum, and maximizing can only weakly raise
it.

\section{Fixed-Span Connectivity Detail}
Let $S$ be a selected interval family with union span $[a,b)$. If the
positive-overlap graph is connected, the union is connected and therefore
covers every elementary segment. At any internal endpoint boundary, a path
from an interval active immediately to its left to one active immediately to
its right must contain an interval crossing the boundary strictly; endpoint
touching alone is not a positive-overlap edge.

Conversely, suppose every elementary segment is covered and every internal
boundary is strictly crossed. Moving from left to right, the crossing interval
at each boundary overlaps intervals covering the adjacent segments with
positive length. Induction joins the segment-covering intervals into one
positive-overlap component. Any selected interval lies inside the covered span
and overlaps the component on a segment it covers, so the full selected family
is connected.

Interleaving segment and boundary rows makes each interval column consecutive.
The lower-coverage, upper-capacity, and bridge constraints are signed copies of
this interval matrix; root and companion fixes are identity rows. The resulting
matrix is totally unimodular with integral right-hand side, proving
Theorem~\ref{thm:oracle}.

\section{Master Nonintegrality Witness}
At capacity two, use four cores
$c_0=[1,2)$, $c_1=[4,7)$, $c_2=[0,2)$, and $c_3=[1,2)$, and five buffers
$b_0=[0,7)$, $b_1=[5,6)$, $b_2=[5,7)$, $b_3=[1,2)$, and $b_4=[2,6)$.
Complete run-column enumeration has LP value four. One fractional solution uses
weights $1/2,1,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2$ on
\[
\{c_0,c_2\},\ \{c_1,b_1\},\ \{c_2,b_3\},\ \{c_3,b_3\},\
\{c_0,b_0,b_4\},\ \{c_3,b_0,b_4\}.
\]
Every core has total coverage one and each buffer has use at most one. Complete
integer enumeration gives value three, attained for example by
$\{c_0,c_2\}$, $\{c_3,b_0,b_1\}$, and $\{c_1,b_4\}$.
